Privacy

Mushaf Reader: the web app at maazarif.github.io/quran-app and its Android and iOS apps. Last updated 12 November 2026.

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. It does not collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your bookmarks, highlights, last page, settings and a short playback log are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing its data) removes them.

What the app downloads

To show pages and play recitation, the app downloads files from these services. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address and basic request details:

  • GitHub Pages: the app itself, the Quran text, fonts and recitation timings
  • jsDelivr: translations and the page layout of the Mushaf
  • MP3Quran, Tarteel, QuranicAudio and EveryAyah: recitation audio
